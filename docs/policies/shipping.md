# Shipping

Draft for business review · 28 October 2026

Your delivery address determines the shipping quote.

## Get a shipping quote

Add your items to the bag and continue on WhatsApp. Share your delivery address so we can confirm whether delivery is available to your location and quote the shipping fee.

Website prices and bag subtotals cover the items only. Shipping is additional. We confirm the shipping charge and final order total on WhatsApp before you pay.

## Delivery arrangements

Delivery options and an estimated timeframe are confirmed on WhatsApp for your address. Do not assume a delivery date until it has been agreed.

Please check your address and contact details before confirming. If they change, contact us promptly so we can discuss the available options.

## Delivery problems

If an order is delayed, damaged or missing, contact us with your order details so we can investigate and discuss the appropriate next steps.

Contact Timeless using the WhatsApp or email link shown on the website.
