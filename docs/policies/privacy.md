# Privacy

Draft for business review · 28 October 2026

How Timeless uses information when you browse, subscribe or request an order.

## Orders and enquiries

When you contact Timeless through WhatsApp or email, you choose the information you share. This may include your name, contact details, delivery address and order details. We use it to answer questions, quote shipping, arrange your order and handle support.

WhatsApp operates separately under its own privacy terms. A bag handoff contains your selected items; you provide your delivery address in the conversation. Do not send card details through this website.

## Email updates

If you choose to subscribe, your email is sent to Brevo to request a confirmation email and manage the mailing list. Confirm using the email link before joining. You can unsubscribe through the link in marketing emails or contact us to request removal.

We use your consent for email marketing. Subscribing is optional and is not a condition of placing an order.

## Browser storage and optional measurement

The website uses local storage to keep your bag and your measurement preference on your device. It may use session storage for introductory animation. You can remove this through your browser settings; doing so may clear your saved bag.

If optional analytics is configured, it loads only after you choose to allow it. You can change that choice using Measurement preferences in the footer. Funnel events describe pages and shopping actions, not your email address or WhatsApp message. Declining measurement does not prevent shopping.

## Service operation

Hosting and service providers process technical information needed to deliver and protect the website. Where configured, error monitoring helps diagnose failures. Newsletter abuse controls briefly retain hashed network and email identifiers in server memory for up to 15 minutes; email content and provider error bodies are not logged by the signup endpoint.

Relevant providers include Brevo for email, Sanity for published website content and WhatsApp for conversations. Optional measurement uses Google Analytics when enabled. Error-only monitoring, when configured, uses Sentry. Providers may process information outside Nigeria under their applicable arrangements.

## Retention and your choices

Order and enquiry information may be needed for fulfilment, support and applicable record-keeping obligations. Contact us to ask about retention, request access or correction, object to processing, withdraw consent or request deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

You may also raise a privacy concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact Timeless using the details below for questions about this notice or your information.
