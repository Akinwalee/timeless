# Returns & order issues

Draft for business review · 28 October 2026

Contact us before sending an item back.

## Ask for help

If you need to discuss a return, exchange or cancellation, contact us on WhatsApp with your order details and the reason for your request. We will discuss the available resolution and any return instructions with you.

Please confirm any voluntary return or exchange conditions before paying for your order. We do not publish a fixed change-of-mind return window here.

## Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

Contact us as soon as you notice an issue. Include your order details, a description and, where useful, photographs so we can assess the problem.

Keep the item and its packaging while the issue is being reviewed. Any agreed return address, shipping arrangements and refund or replacement steps will be confirmed directly with you.

## Your rights

Nothing in this information limits any consumer rights or remedies that apply to your purchase under applicable law.

Contact Timeless using the WhatsApp or email link shown on the website.
